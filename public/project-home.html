ABOUT THE TEAM

POLARIS is an independent engineering-driven team participating in NASA Space Apps 2026. We bring together electrical power-system modeling, simulation, space-science data analysis and explainable AI-assisted workflows. Our public prototype compares lunar south-pole reference sites and mission schedule changes, evaluates a preliminary photovoltaic–battery energy budget and documents the assumptions behind each result. POLARIS is a participant project, not a NASA-endorsed mission-planning system.

ABOUT THE CHALLENGE

Near the Moon’s south pole, low-angle sunlight and rugged terrain can obstruct local views of the Sun and Earth. A mission delay or a change in electrical demand can alter the operating conditions of a candidate site. The challenge is to make NASA observations easier to compare: sites, dates, local-horizon geometry and the potential for solar power generation. POLARIS combines NASA-derived geometry with a simplified energy model and transparent scenario assessment. Earth visibility is a geometric estimate, not a verified direct-to-Earth radio communication window.
