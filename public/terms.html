Terms & Privacy Notice

POLARIS stores your account request so an administrator can decide whether you may access the private mission workspace.

Data collected

We collect your first name, last name, professional email through the authentication provider, your employee or engineer identifier, the acceptance time of these terms and the status of your registration request. When you sign in, the identity provider stores session creation timestamps for security and access review.

How data is used

Your details are used for access accreditation, ownership attribution on mission plans and engineering reports, and administrator review of the platform. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are not available to POLARIS administrators.

Access controls

An administrator must approve your request before you can use protected missions. Multi-factor verification is required for private missions. Administrators can see submitted names, staff identifiers, application and session times, and approval status. Accounts are not transferable.

Your responsibilities

Provide accurate details, do not share your credentials, protect your authenticator and report unauthorized access promptly. Public NASA reference data are available separately in demo mode.

Requests about your data

Contact the project administrator to request corrections or account closure. This technical pilot is not an official NASA authentication service and does not accept restricted NASA mission data.
