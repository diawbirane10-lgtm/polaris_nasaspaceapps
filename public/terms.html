Workspace terms and privacy notice

POLARIS is a demonstration mission-planning platform. Registration is subject to review and does not guarantee access.

Information collected

First and last name, professional ID, email (managed by the authentication provider), registration and approval status, dates of access, mission configurations and generated reports.

Use of information

To authenticate users, maintain engineering audit records, protect private mission simulations, and provide an access-history dashboard for authorized reviewers.

Access and security

Authorized reviewers see name, professional ID, registration status and connection dates, not passwords or access tokens. Account security uses multifactor authentication and database access policies. Data is hosted by Supabase in the European Union region.

Your choices

Do not enter classified or otherwise restricted information. For correction or deletion requests contact the project owner. Mission data will be handled according to the policies defined by the POLARIS operating team.
