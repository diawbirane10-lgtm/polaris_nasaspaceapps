## Project summary (ready for review, English)
POLARIS is a scientific software demonstrator for the NASA Space Apps 2026 Lunar Landing Site Navigator challenge. It compares locations and dates near the lunar south pole using public NASA elevation data and observer ephemerides. The interface shows the local skyline and apparent Sun/Earth paths, explores scheduling delays and alternative sites, and provides a simplified battery/power scenario. It also documents its evidence and uncertainties, making complex mission trade-offs easier to explore for educators and mission-planning audiences. POLARIS is not a certified mission planner or an RF-link-availability predictor.

## Sources (all four required groups + independent check)
- LROC QuickMap: https://quickmap.lroc.asu.edu/ (visual exploration and inspection)
- NASA Moon Trek: https://trek.nasa.gov/moon/ (south-pole reference WMTS layers; aggregate context, not date-specific mission predictions)
- JPL Horizons: https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/ (site-specific apparent azimuth/elevation)
- NASA GSFC LOLA / planetary geodesy: https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/78 (5 m/pixel DEM and local skyline)
- NAIF SPICE: https://naif.jpl.nasa.gov/ (cross-check on observer ephemeris angles)

## Actual evidence
- 58 Python automated tests passed in local run on Nov 14, 2026.
- At 57 six-hour samples, two bodies and two sites, SPICE/JPL angular output agrees within ~1e-6 degree. These approaches share ephemeris heritage; result is **algorithm agreement**, not independent physical proof.
- Repeating a site comparison with 1h JPL rather than 6h JPL observations changed a sample Shackleton Rim 24h Earth-clear estimate from 18h to 21h. Use the 1h figure only in the sampling audit. The integrated offline counterfactual demo still uses 6h observations.
- The terrain horizon includes only a 6 km radius, 5-degree azimuth and 50 m radial bins, so long-distance terrain is unassessed.
- Lunar Earth-clear potential is **not** an operational communications window; no Earth station or antenna RF link budget is modeled.
- PV/battery results are approximate scenario calculations; no thermal/radiation/full EPS qualification.
